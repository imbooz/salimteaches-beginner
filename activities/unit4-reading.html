My Family

This is my mother, Anna. She's fifty-two years old. This is my father, Peter. He's fifty-four years old.

I have one brother, Tom. He's twenty-eight. Tom's wife is Julia, and their daughter is only two months old! Her name is Emma.

My grandmother's name is Rose. She's eighty years old. She lives with my parents.

Lucy needs her umbrella because it's raining outside.

Jack's Grandparents

Every Sunday, Jack visits his grandparents' house. He sits in front of the house with his grandfather, and they talk about football.
