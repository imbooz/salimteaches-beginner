TEXT 1

At the Hotel

Anna is at a hotel. She has a reservation. She says, “Excuse me, I have a reservation.”

The receptionist asks, “What’s your name, please?” Anna says, “My name is Anna Brown.” The receptionist asks, “How do you spell your last name?” Anna says, “B-R-O-W-N.”

The receptionist says, “Thank you, Ms Brown. You are in room 14.” Anna takes her key and goes to her room. There is a bed, a desk, a lamp, a chair, a television and a phone in the room.

1. Annaning familiyasi nima?
2. Annada nima bor?
3. Annaning xona raqami nechchi?
4. Annaning xonasida bo‘lgan bitta narsani yozing.
5. Anna nimani oladi?
TEXT 2

At the Café

Tom is in a café. He says, “I’d like a coffee, please.”

The waiter asks, “Would you like black or white?” Tom says, “Black, please.”

Tom also wants a croissant and orange juice. His friend Sara wants an omelet and still mineral water. The waiter brings their food and drinks and says, “Here you are.”

6. Tom birinchi bo‘lib nimani buyurtma qiladi?
7. Tom qahvasini qanday ichishni xohlaydi?
8. Tom qahvasi bilan yana nima xohlaydi?
9. Sara nima ichishni xohlaydi?
10. Ofitsiant buyurtmani olib kelganda nima deydi?
TEXT 3

In the Room

Ben is in his hotel room. He looks at the desk and says, “This is a desk.” He points to a lamp near the desk and says, “That is a lamp.”

There are two chairs near the television. Ben says, “These are chairs.” Then he points to the keys on the desk. “Those are my keys.”

Ben puts his phone on the desk. The phone is here, but the television is there. He sits on a chair and opens his notebook.

11. Ben “This is a desk” deydi, chunki stol unga yaqin.
12. Ben “Those are my keys” deydi, chunki bir nechta kalit bor.
13. Televizor yonida bitta stul bor.
14. Ben telefon shu yerda, deydi.
15. Ben daftarni ochadi.
